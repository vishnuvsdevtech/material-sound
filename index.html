Four materials from two primitives: white noise through a filter (paper, the glass attack) and a damped sine at a chosen frequency (wood, metal). A wood knock is a fast decay at 180 Hz plus a noise transient; a bell is the same sine with a forty-times longer tail and two detuned partials beating against each other.

Sound is off by default and the toggle is visible, because an interface that makes noise without permission is a bug. 40 ms is the threshold where a click stops feeling like a click and starts feeling like material.
